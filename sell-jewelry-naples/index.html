What helps us make an accurate offer?

Photos should show the entire item, any hallmarks, designer signatures, gemstones, and accompanying paperwork. If you know the metal, brand, purchase history, or whether a stone has a grading report, please let us know. You do not need to have this information to request an evaluation.

Sell without pressure

Visit the showroom during business hours, or arrange a private appointment in Naples and the surrounding Southwest Florida area. The evaluation is free, and you retain complete control over your decision. If an offer is not right for you, there is no obligation to sell.
