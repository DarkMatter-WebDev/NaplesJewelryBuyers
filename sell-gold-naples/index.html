What gold items can I sell?

We evaluate gold jewelry in any condition, including broken chains, single earrings, rings, bracelets, pendants, and unwanted pieces. We also evaluate gold coins, bars, and bullion. Bring together everything you are considering; evaluating several pieces at once can make the process easier.

Gold Jewelry

Scrap Gold

Broken, bent, outdated, or mismatched gold jewelry can still be evaluated for its gold content.

Coins & Bullion

Gold coins, bars, and collections are evaluated individually, with collector value considered where applicable.

How we evaluate gold

An offer considers the item’s gold purity, weight, and the current precious-metal market. Some pieces may have value beyond melt weight because of diamonds, gemstones, designer marks, rarity, or condition. Tell us about anything you know about the piece and include clear photos if you submit an online estimate request.
